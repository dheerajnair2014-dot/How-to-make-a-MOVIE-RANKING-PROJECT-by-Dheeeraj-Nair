The Best Movies According to Dheeru

My top 3 movies of all-time.

Bethleham Kudumba Unit (2026)

A Malayalam feel-good romantic comedy-drama directed by Girish A.D. and written by Girish A.D. and Kiran Josey. The Film Stars Nivin Pauly as Justin and Mamitha Baiju as Ashley. The story follows Ashley, a college student/young woman who moves with her family to Angamaly (Ernakulam district), and Justin, an older neighbor in their local church community ("Bethlehem Kudumba Unit"). The film explores a non-creepy, mature, May-December age-gap romance, anchored by everyday family dynamics, music connections, and local community life. Released theatrically on August 21, 2026, and premiered on JioHotstar on October 2, 2026.It is the highest- grossing Malayalam film of 2026 surpassing Lokah Chapter 1: Chandra (2025).

GOAT (Greatest Of All Time) (2024)

The Tamil action-thriller The Greatest of All Time (GOAT), directed by Venkat Prabhu and starring Vijay, tells the story of MS Gandhi, a former member of the elite Special Anti-Terrorism Squad (SATS). Gandhi and his SATS team (including partners played by Prabhu Deva, Prashanth, and Ajmal Ameer) are involved in operations tracking stolen uranium and rogue operatives like Rajiv Menon (Mohan). During a critical mission in Kenya/Thailand, Gandhi's young son Jeevan is seemingly lost or killed due to fallout from these conflicts, leading to a split between Gandhi and his wife Anu (Sneha). 7 years later, an older Gandhi crosses paths with a young man claiming to be his long-lost son, Jeevan. In the climax, it is revealed that the "Jeevan" who infiltrated Gandhi's life and worked against him was not Gandhi's real biological son, but rather a clone created and manipulated by Rajiv Menon to destroy Gandhi and tarnish SATS. In the final moments of the film, a firing range scene shows a clean-shaven Vijay alongside another clone of Jeevan, hinting that more clones exist and setting up a potential sequel (GOAT vs OG).

Leo (2023)

Directed by Lokesh Kanagaraj and starring Thalapathy Vijay, Leo (2023) is an action-thriller inspired by David Cronenberg's 2005 film A History of Violence.• Vijay plays Parthi, a quiet cafe owner and animal rescuer living a peaceful, low-profile life with his wife Sathya and their children in Theog, Himachal Pradesh. Vijay plays Parthi, a quiet cafe owner and animal rescuer living a peaceful, low-profile life with his wife Sathya and their children in Theog, Himachal Pradesh. After Parthi brutally fights off a group of armed gangsters who try to rob his cafe, a video of his lethal combat skills goes viral. This display of extreme violence attracts the attention of a ruthless drug syndicate led by Antony Das (Sanjay Dutt) and his brother Harold Das (Arjun Sarja). They believe Parthi is actually Leo Das—Antony's estranged son—who was supposedly dead. The film explores a dark family history involving a criminal empire and a ritualistic/violent background. Throughout the movie, Parthi desperately tries to protect his family and denies being Leo to preserve his quiet life. However, after a violent confrontation where he kills Antony Das, Parthi finally drops the act, looks his dying father in the eye, and admits that he is indeed Leo Das.
